Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Operator: Hopalt · Contact: hopalt@gmail.com

Super Foam Canvas is designed for children. It does not require an account. The Android app may display child-appropriate contextual banner ads through Unity Ads and does not use behavioral advertising or cross-device tracking. The iOS app has no ads.

1. Information we collect

Super Foam Canvas does not ask users to create an account or sign in. Hopalt does not intentionally collect a user's name, email address, precise location, contacts, artwork, or drawing activity through the app. When advertising is shown, Unity Ads may automatically process the limited technical information described in Section 6.

2. Artwork stored on the device

Artwork and related save information are stored locally on the user's device. They are not uploaded to Hopalt. Users can delete artwork from My Art or remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app.

3. Saving and sharing

If a user selects Save to Photos, the artwork is exported to the device's photo library. If a user selects Share, the file is passed to the operating system share sheet and then to an app chosen by the user. The privacy policy of that selected service applies after the file is shared. Photos exported to the photo library must be deleted separately in the device's Photos app.

4. Network access

The app may connect to the internet to retrieve its coloring catalog and images and, when enabled, request and display advertising. Network infrastructure may process limited technical information, such as an IP address and basic request logs, to deliver and secure content and advertising. Hopalt does not use artwork or drawing activity for advertising or profiling.

5. Children's privacy

The app is intended for children and families. Hopalt does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the app. If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected unintentionally, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

6. Advertising and Unity Ads

The Android app may display banner ads through the Unity Ads SDK provided by Unity Technologies. The iOS app has no ads. The app is designated as directed to children in Unity Ads, so only contextual ads are requested, not behavioral (personalized) ads, and the app does not request access to the Android advertising ID. To deliver and operate ads and to prevent fraud, Unity Ads may process approximate location, device and other identifiers, views and taps while an ad is shown, and app diagnostics; this data is encrypted in transit. Unity may process this information outside the user's country under its own safeguards. See the Unity Privacy Policy for details about Unity's data retention, deletion, and privacy rights.

7. Other third-party services

Device operating-system features used for photo export and sharing are provided by the platform operator and selected third-party apps under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Security and retention

Because artwork remains on the device, its security and retention are affected by the user's device settings, backups, photo library, and selected sharing services. Hopalt does not retain a server-side copy of the artwork. Advertising-related information is retained by Unity according to its policy and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, advertising configuration, or other services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revision is published.

10. Contact

For privacy or support questions about Super Foam Canvas, email hopalt@gmail.com.
